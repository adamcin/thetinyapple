Every Love Story Deserves to be Told

Hello, I'm Megan

I'm a wedding and portrait photographer based in Manzanita, Oregon, available for travel worldwide. I believe that the most powerful photographs are the ones that capture genuine emotion — the unscripted glances, the nervous laughter, the quiet in-between moments that make your story uniquely yours.

With a background in fine art and a love for natural light, I approach every session as a collaboration. My goal is for you to feel at ease so the real you can shine through every frame.

Life is all about people, moments & emotions that we want to hold on to.

As a wedding photographer I know how important it is to capture every beautiful moment of your day. I specialize in natural, candid images that tell the story of your love — not just the posed portraits, but the real, unguarded moments in between.

Whether you're planning an intimate elopement on the Oregon coast, a grand ballroom affair, or a destination wedding in Tuscany, I'll be there to document it all with care, artistry, and a whole lot of heart.
